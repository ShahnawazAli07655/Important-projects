Python4Physics 2025

Computational Physics at UC Berkeley

This repository contains my report from the Python4Physics 2025 programme at the University of California, Berkeley.

The 10-day programme explored the application of Python and computational methods to problems in modern physics, covering topics ranging from particle physics and astrophysics to gravitational waves and condensed matter physics.

Topics Covered

- Python fundamentals for physics
- Statistics and data analysis
- Astrophysics and Cosmic Microwave Background
- Neutron decay simulation
- Breit–Wigner distribution and resonance phenomena
- Gravitational-wave modelling
- Ising model and spin systems
- Calculus and differential equations
- Standard Model of Particle Physics
- Axions and dark matter
